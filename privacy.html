Complete Home Today LLC ("Complete Home Today," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through completehomellc.com, how we use it, and the rights you have — including rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA).

Information We Collect

  • Information you give us: name, phone number, email address, home address, and details about the security products or services you're interested in, when you submit a quote or contact form.
  • Automatically collected: basic device and usage data (such as IP address, browser type, and pages viewed) via standard web technologies and cookies.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your request, prepare a quote, and schedule installation.
  • To contact you about home security products and services — by phone, text (SMS), and email — consistent with the consent you provide.
  • To operate, improve, and secure our website.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

Calls & Texts (TCPA)

When you submit a form and consent, you agree that Complete Home Today and Direct Protection Security (an ADT Authorized Dealer) may contact you at the number provided, including by autodialed or pre-recorded calls and text messages. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out of texts by replying STOP, and you can ask us to stop calling at any time.

How We Share Information

We share the information you submit with Direct Protection Security (DPS), an ADT Authorized Dealer, and its affiliated installation and monitoring providers, so they can fulfill your request. We may also share information with service providers who support our website and operations, and where required by law. We do not sell your personal information for money.

Your California Privacy Rights & "Do Not Sell or Share"

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose;
  • Request deletion of your personal information;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information;
  • Opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information (including sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising); and
  • Not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights.

To exercise any of these rights — including to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information — email darrell@completehomellc.com or call (408) 909-4484 with your request. We will verify and respond as required by law.

Data Retention & Security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.
